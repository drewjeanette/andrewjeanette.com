Delete your Canon account

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Canon, by Andrew Jeanette, works without an account. If you created the optional sync account, you can delete it and all data stored for it at any time.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Canon on Android, iOS or the web, and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Delete by email

If you can’t use the app, email andrewjeanettebusiness@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Canon account.” We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and password) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Everything synced for your account: notes and drawings, bookmarks, personal translations, your canon and book order, and reading progress.

Deletion is immediate and permanent, and nothing is kept on our servers. Copies on your own devices stay until you uninstall the app or clear its data. See the privacy policy for details.
