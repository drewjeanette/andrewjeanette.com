Privacy Policy for Canon

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Canon (“Canon,” “the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use it on Android, iOS or the web at andrewjeanette.com/projects/canon. Canon is developed and maintained by Andrew Jeanette.

Summary

Canon is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. You can use every feature without an account, and then everything stays on your device. If you choose to create an optional account, we store your email address and your Canon data so it can sync between your devices. We never sell or share it, and you can delete it at any time.

Without an account

Your canon, bookmarks, reading progress, notes and drawings, and personal translations are stored only on your device. We have no access to them. Uninstalling the app (or clearing site data on the web) removes them.

With an optional account

If you sign in, we collect and store:

  • Your email address and password — handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your password in hashed form. We never see your password.
  • Your Canon data — notes and drawings, bookmarks, personal translations, your canon and book order, and reading progress, stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare so it can sync between your devices.

We use this information only to sign you in and to back up and sync your data. We do not sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising. All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase Authentication (sign-in): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Cloudflare (hosting and the sync database). Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google Play and the App Store deliver the app and may collect information such as crash reports and install statistics under their own policies, for example policies.google.com/privacy.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your login and everything stored for your account on our servers. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app. If you can’t use the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days. Step-by-step instructions are on the account deletion page.

Children’s privacy

Canon is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party content

Canon contains religious, historical and literary texts provided for education and personal study. Canon is not an official publication of any church, denomination or religious organization, and including a text does not imply endorsement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
