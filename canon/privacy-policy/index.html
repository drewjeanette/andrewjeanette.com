Privacy Policy for Canon

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Canon mobile application (“Canon,” “the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use it. Canon is developed and maintained by Andrew Jeanette.

By using Canon, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary

Canon is designed as a privacy-friendly reading and study app. We do not require you to create an account, we do not collect personal information, and your notes, drawings, and custom translations stay on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

Canon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. Specifically, we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any account information, because Canon does not use accounts or user logins.

Information Stored On Your Device

Some features of Canon create data that is saved locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Notes and drawings — any text notes or freehand drawings you add to chapters.
  • Custom translations — any personal translations or renderings you create and save.
  • App preferences and library settings — such as which canon collections or content packs you have selected or purchased.

This information is stored on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not have access to it. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device. We do not provide a cloud backup service for this content, so you are responsible for any backups you wish to keep (for example, through your device’s own backup features).

In-App Purchases

Canon offers optional in-app purchases for additional text packs and non-canonical content. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details, such as your credit card number or billing address.

Google processes purchase transactions according to its own privacy policy. Google may share with us limited, non-identifying information about purchases (such as which product was bought) so that the app can deliver the content you paid for. For more information about how Google handles your data, please review the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Play Services

Because Canon is distributed through the Google Play Store, Google may collect certain information automatically as part of operating the Play Store and app delivery, including for purchases, crash reporting, and basic app statistics. This collection is governed by Google’s privacy policy, not ours. You can learn more at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children’s Privacy

Canon does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information from any user, it does not collect personal information from children either. The app is intended for general audiences interested in religious, historical, and educational texts.

Third-Party Content

Canon contains religious, historical, and literary texts provided for educational and personal study. Canon is not an official publication of any church, denomination, or religious organization, and the inclusion of any text does not imply endorsement.

Data Security

Because your notes, drawings, and custom translations are stored only on your device, their security depends on the security of your device. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or other security measures.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of Canon after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
